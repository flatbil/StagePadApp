## Description (4000 characters max)

MD Buddy is a live performance controller built for Music Directors who run backing tracks and live sets in Ableton Live.

Connect your iPad to the MD Buddy Bridge running on your Mac, and your entire set appears instantly — every song, every section, laid out in one view. Tap a section to queue a jump. It fires on the next bar, never mid-phrase.

**Designed for the stage**
- See all your songs and sections at a glance
- Tap any section to jump there — quantized to the bar so it always lands in time
- Reorder your setlist with a long press and drag, right before you walk on stage
- PLAY and STOP controls always within reach
- Works over Wi-Fi or USB — no internet required

**Built for worship and live performance**
Whether you're leading a Sunday morning service, a rehearsal, or a touring show, MD Buddy keeps you in control without pulling your attention away from the room.

**Automatic connection**
MD Buddy finds the bridge on your Mac automatically via Bonjour — no IP addresses to type, no setup at the venue. Plug in or connect to the same Wi-Fi and it's ready.

**Cue point driven**
Structure your Ableton set with cue point markers and MD Buddy reads them automatically. Name your cue points with == Song Name == for song headers and section names like Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge for the rest. That's all the setup required.

**Requires the free MD Buddy Bridge**
MD Buddy works alongside the MD Buddy Bridge — a free companion app for Mac that connects to Ableton Live via AbletonOSC. Available at github.com/flatbil/AbletonTracksApp.

## Notes for reviewer (App Review Information)

**Demo account:** Not required — no login or account needed.

**Review notes:**
MD Buddy connects to a companion Mac application (MD Buddy Bridge) running on the same local network. For review purposes, the app will display a "Connecting..." state if no bridge is present — this is expected behaviour and not a crash. The app is fully functional when the bridge is running on a Mac with Ableton Live.

The bridge is open source and available at: https://github.com/flatbil/AbletonTracksApp

## Privacy Policy (minimal — can be hosted as a GitHub README section)

MD Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app communicates exclusively with the MD Buddy Bridge running on the user's own local network. No data leaves the local network. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party services are used.
